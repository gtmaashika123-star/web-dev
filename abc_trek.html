ABC Trek

The Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) Trek is one of the most popular and scenic trekking routes in Nepal. The trek takes visitors through beautiful forests, terraced fields, traditional Gurung and Magar villages, and stunning Himalayan landscapes. The journey leads to Annapurna Base Camp, which is located at an altitude of 4,130 meters. Trekkers can enjoy spectacular views of the Annapurna mountain range, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), Dhaulagiri, and other Himalayan peaks. The trek also provides an opportunity to experience Nepal's natural beauty, mountain culture, and local lifestyle.
