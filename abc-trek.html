ABC Trek

The Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) trek is a world-renowned, moderate-level journey into the heart of Nepal's Himalayas that peaks at a high-altitude glacial basin sitting at 4,130 meters (13,545 feet). Spanning 7 to 12 days and covering roughly 70 to 135 kilometers, the trail winds through an incredible diversity of landscapes, shifting rapidly from lush rhododendron forests and terraced emerald farmlands to deep river gorges and alpine fields. Along the route, hikers sleep in local teahouses and experience the rich hospitality of the Gurung and Magar ethnic communities in villages like Ghandruk and Chhomrong.
